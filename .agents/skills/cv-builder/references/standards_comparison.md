# UK Curriculum Vitae vs. US Resume Comparison Guide

Understanding the geographical and procedural differences between a **UK CV** (governed by Prospects and the National Careers Service) and a **US Resume** (exemplified by Harvard FAS standards) is crucial for tailoring applications accurately.

---

## 1. Key Structural Differences

| Criteria | UK Curriculum Vitae (Prospects / NCS) | US Resume (Harvard FAS) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Document Name** | Curriculum Vitae (CV) | Résumé / Resume |
| **Document Title on Page** | Candidate's Full Name (Never write "CV") | Candidate's Full Name (Never write "Resume") |
| **Typical Length** | **Up to 2 full A4 pages** (Standard)<br>*Academic/Medical can be 3+ pages* | **Strictly 1 page** (Undergrad/Recent Grad)<br>*Max 2 pages only for 10+ years experience* |
| **Paper Standard** | A4 (210 × 297 mm) | US Letter (8.5 × 11 inches) |
| **Personal Profile / Summary** | **Standard Practice**: 3–4 sentence Personal Statement at the top highlighting sector aims. | **Rare / Optional**: Skipped by most college & early-career candidates to conserve space for experience. |
| **Education vs Experience Order** | Education first for students/new graduates; Experience first once working for 2+ years. | Education first for undergraduates; Experience first for alumni and experienced hires. |
| **Grades / Classification** | Degree classifications (e.g., First Class, 2:1, 2:2) and A-Levels/GCSE summaries. | GPA (only if 3.5 or above on 4.0 scale), Latin honors (cum laude), and relevant coursework. |
| **Interests Section** | Accepted if relevant and demonstrating transferable soft skills/extracurricular leadership. | Usually compressed into a 1–2 line "Skills & Interests" section at the very bottom. |
| **References** | "References available upon request" or omitted to save space. | **Strictly omitted**. Employers request references separately during offer stages. |
| **Cover Letter Companion** | Expected for almost all professional applications unless explicitly stated otherwise. | Expected for most corporate, finance, tech, and non-profit internship/full-time applications. |

---

## 2. Universal Standards (Shared by Both)

Both UK and US elite standards strictly enforce:
1. **No Personal Details**: Never include photograph, date of birth (DOB), marital status, gender, or nationality.
2. **Reverse Chronological Order**: Recent jobs and qualifications first.
3. **Third-Person Elimination**: No "I", "me", "my", "we", or "our".
4. **Action-Verb Bullet Points**: Every bullet begins with an active verb.
5. **Quantifiable Outcomes**: Focus on metrics, percentage improvements, revenue generated, hours saved, and scale managed.
6. **Error-Free Proofreading**: Zero tolerance for grammatical or typographical errors.

---

## 3. Which One Should You Build?

### Choose a **UK CV** if:
- You are applying for jobs, graduate schemes, or internships in the **United Kingdom**, Ireland, or countries following British Commonwealth conventions.
- You are applying for **academic, research, scientific, or medical** posts worldwide.
- You have substantial diverse experience that naturally fits 2 balanced pages without fluff.

### Choose a **US Resume** if:
- You are applying to companies based in the **United States**, Canada, or multinational tech corporations that utilize US-style applicant screening.
- The application platform limits uploads to a single-page document.
- You are a current student or recent graduate targeting competitive US industries (investment banking, management consulting, software engineering).
